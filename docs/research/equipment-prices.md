# Equipment prices (without installation)

Our own research (WeShape, 14 November 2026). The report shows the **equipment price only**;
installation is not in our price (it depends on the company, or the person does it). The expert's
section 11 gives **installed** prices; these are shop prices for the equipment alone, mostly Europe,
incl. VAT, November 2026. **Rough ranges for the report**, to be scaled by country (price level and
import tariffs, see `docs/decisions/2026-11-12-prices-as-ranges.md`) and refreshed yearly.
