# The PDF report: what it shows and how it looks

**Date:** 14 November 2026. **Agreed with:** Timi. Status: design only, not built (needs the scoring).

## On every page

- **Header:** the exact address of the place and its coordinates (e.g. "46.7712° N · 23.5897° E"), so the
  report points to one place only.

- **Watermark:** "WeShape Energy", diagonal, light, behind the content.
- **Footer:** date made, **valid until** (one year later), page number.

## Pages

1. **Summary (the most important page)**
   - The **3 best options, ranked** by the person's priority. For each:
     - **equipment price** in the local currency, as a range ("about from–to");
     - **installation is not in the price**, with the text: it depends on the company that installs
       it, or the person can do it; WeShape does not calculate it;
     - yearly saving and payback, as ranges;
     - CO₂ saved a year.
   - **What was missing** (empty fields and "Don't know" answers), so the result is less precise.
2. **The home, briefly:** building, insulation, use, energy sources, power cuts.
3. **The best option:** how strongly we recommend it (a percentage) and why; monthly production as a
   small bar chart; how much is used at home; battery advice; what it needs (permit, whether a
   professional is required, upkeep). Tenants only get options that suit tenants, and the page says
   so ("this suits you as a tenant").
4. **Comparison, briefly:** all options, including those that did not come out on top, with why.
5. **Last page:** "This is an estimate, so prices are ranges"; how long it is valid ("prices and data
   are updated every year; for new values, make a new report in the app"); what we calculated from,
   with the expert values credited to WeShape Energy on their own line. **No QR code** (decided after
   the samples).

## Watermark

Over the box backgrounds and under all content (text, numbers, chart, QR), so it shows across the
whole page without covering anything (agreed after the first sample).
